Alex Michaels

Musical Theatre Performer · Singer · Actor

Alex Michaels is a Hamburg-based musical theatre performer, singer, and actor with over 20 years of stage experience. Conservatory-trained, with credits spanning regional musical theatre, six seasons with AIDA Cruises, and a current tour with The 12 Tenors, Alex brings range, discipline, and a distinct international perspective to every stage.
